Frequently Asked Questions (5 FAQs)

Learn more about cosmetic breast lift, breast reshaping surgery, and breast firmness restoration with our Jersey City Plastic Surgeon.

A cosmetic breast lift (medically known as mastopexy surgery) is a procedure designed to raise and rejuvenate sagging breasts by removing excess stretched skin and tightening surrounding breast tissue. Ideal candidates are healthy non-smokers who are bothered by downward-pointing nipples, stretched skin after pregnancy or weight loss, or loss of upper-pole fullness, and who wish to restore a perkier, more youthful contour.

Breast reshaping surgery allows your Plastic Surgeon at Breast Lift NJ to carefully sculpt internal breast tissue, refine areola size and placement, and balance differences in height or volume between the left and right breasts. By customizing incision patterns to your unique anatomy, the procedure creates symmetrical, naturally proportioned curves tailored to your frame.

Yes. Many patients achieve substantial breast firmness restoration through mastopexy surgery alone by tightening lax skin and elevating existing glandular tissue into a higher, firmer position. However, if you also desire increased cup volume or enhanced upper-pole fullness, your Plastic Surgeon can combine breast lift surgery with breast implants during a single procedure.

Most patients return to desk work and light daily routines within 5 to 7 days after breast lift surgery, while wearing a supportive surgical garment to protect healing tissues. Strenuous exercise and heavy lifting are typically resumed after 4 to 6 weeks. Swelling steadily subsides over the first few months as your final elevated contour settles.
